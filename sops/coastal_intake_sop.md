# Coastal DME — Workers' Compensation Intake Standard Operating Procedures
**Version 2.3 · Owner: Operations Lead · Change control: review & approve**

## 1. Scope
These procedures govern intake of durable medical equipment (DME) referrals for injured workers under workers' compensation. They are the authoritative source for how a referral is validated, completed, and routed.

## 2. Authorization
2.1 No DME referral may be scheduled or dispatched without an **active authorization reference** on file from the claim's carrier/adjuster. If the authorization reference is missing, request it from the adjuster and **hold the episode** until it is received.

## 3. Diagnosis Coding
3.4 When the referral form, clinical notes, and prescription **disagree on the ICD-10 diagnosis code**, the treating physician's **clinical notes (corroborated by the prescription) are authoritative**. Adopt the clinical code and document the override.
3.7 For DME following a **traumatic injury and surgery**, code the **acute injury encounter (S-codes)**, not the degenerative-condition code (M-codes).

## 4. Coverage & Equipment
4.2 A mobility device may only be routed when a **qualifying mobility-limiting diagnosis** is on file per the applicable CMS Local Coverage Determination.
4.5 **Confirm patient weight before dispatch.** Patients over 300 lbs require a **bariatric unit**; never dispatch a standard unit where a bariatric specification applies.

## 5. Jurisdiction & Turnaround
5.2 Apply the **state turnaround clock** to every referral. **Texas referrals must be actioned within 2 business days**; other states per their fee-schedule SLA. Prioritize and **escalate any episode at risk of breaching** its SLA.

## 6. Autonomy & Confidence
6.1 **Auto-route** an episode only when extraction confidence is **90% or higher**. **Spot-check** episodes at 80–90%. **Escalate any episode below 80%** to a specialist.

## 7. Language & Accommodation
7.1 Capture the injured worker's **primary language and interpreter need** on every intake; **coordinate an interpreter** for any non-English speaker before the appointment.

## 8. Escalation
8.0 **Clinical and coding judgment is never fully automated.** Any ambiguous diagnosis, split evidence, or laterality conflict must be **routed to a specialist** with full documentation. The coordinator — or the agent — **does not guess.**
