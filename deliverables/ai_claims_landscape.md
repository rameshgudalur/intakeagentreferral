# AI-Native Companies for Medical Health-Insurance Payers — Claims Landscape

*Compiled June 2026. **Scope: US medical health plans** (commercial / Medicare Advantage / Medicaid) — i.e., the **payer side** of medical claims. Excludes provider-side RCM/coding/intake, patient/consumer tools, and workers'-compensation. AI-first companies only (legacy incumbents such as Optum, Cotiviti, and Change/Optum's older tools are excluded). Funding, clients, and positioning are point-in-time.*

**Legend — *Managed services?***: **Yes** = runs the process for the plan (managed / BPaaS) · **Hybrid** = software option **and** a managed/delegated option · **No** = software the plan operates. *Clients are publicly disclosed; "not public" = customers exist but names aren't disclosed.*

## 1 · Prior authorization & utilization management
- **Cohere Health** — category leader for PA/UM; auto-approves up to ~90% of requests; ~12M PA requests/yr; expanding into payment integrity.
  - *Managed services:* **Hybrid** — in-house software + delegated end-to-end managed option (Cohere Complete™).
  - *Clients:* **Humana** (national MA + Commercial), **Geisinger Health Plan**, **CMS**; ~660K providers on platform.
- **Anterior** (fmr. Co:Helm) — AI for health-plan back office: prior auth, payment integrity, risk adjustment; $40M raised.
  - *Managed services:* **No / unconfirmed** — payer back-office automation.
  - *Clients:* **Geisinger Health Plan** (others not public).
- **Basys.ai** — enterprise generative AI spanning **prior auth, UM, and payment integrity** for health plans.
  - *Managed services:* **No** — genAI automation platform.
  - *Clients:* health plans (names not public).
- **Itiliti Health** — prior-authorization automation for payers (API/interoperability, CMS-0057 compliance).
  - *Managed services:* **No** — software/API platform.
  - *Clients:* payers and providers (names largely not public).

## 2 · Payment integrity & payment accuracy
- **Machinify** — unified pre-pay + post-pay payment-integrity platform across the claim lifecycle.
  - *Managed services:* **Hybrid** — platform + recovery services (model not fully public).
  - *Clients:* national/regional health plans (names not public).
- **Codoxo** — genAI "Unified Cost Containment" platform; "Point Zero" intervenes **pre-claim** (vs. post-pay audit); covers 80M+ lives. $69M raised ($35M Series C led by **CVS Health Ventures**, Dec '25).
  - *Managed services:* **Hybrid** — platform spanning provider education, prepay editing, fraud detection, audit, medical-record review.
  - *Clients:* large US health plans (CVS Health Ventures-backed; specific names not public).
- **Lyric** (lyric.ai) — AI payment-accuracy for health plans; reviews >50% of US claims (~190M Americans); Fast Company Most Innovative 2026. *(Note: AI-forward but has legacy roots in ClaimsXten.)*
  - *Managed services:* **Hybrid** — payment-accuracy platform + services.
  - *Clients:* major national health plans (covers ~190M lives; specific names not public).
- **ClarisHealth** — Pareo® payment-integrity platform for health plans (recoveries + admin-cost reduction).
  - *Managed services:* **No** — enterprise software platform the plan operates.
  - *Clients:* regional & national health plans (names not public).
- **Rialtic** — AI clinical & payment-policy engine for payers (payment integrity / claims editing). *(Lighter public detail.)*
  - *Managed services:* **Hybrid** — policy/editing platform.
  - *Clients:* health plans (not public).

## 3 · Claims operations & adjudication automation
- **Alaffia Health** — AI + expert clinicians for **end-to-end health-plan claims ops** (UM, payment integrity, appeals); $55M Series B (Feb '26). The clearest AI-native BPaaS for payers.
  - *Managed services:* **Yes** — offers **both** software subscriptions **and managed services**; runs bill/chart reviews on plans' behalf.
  - *Clients:* Medicaid, Medicare Advantage & commercial plans (up to ~2M members), TPAs, cost-containment firms, government agencies.
- **ClaimSphere** — AI engine that auto-adjudicates claims against payer-specific benefit logic, clinical edits, and COB with no human touch.
  - *Managed services:* **No** — adjudication automation engine.
  - *Clients:* payers (names not public).
- **Amera** — automates claims processing for payers: turns messy PDF / EDI / non-standard submissions into structured, system-ready data.
  - *Managed services:* **No** — claims-processing software.
  - *Clients:* health-insurance payers (names not public).
- **Curacel** — AI claims processing/automation & fraud detection for payors and TPAs *(strong in emerging markets — lower fit for a US-only scan).*
  - *Managed services:* **No** — claims infrastructure/software.
  - *Clients:* payers, TPAs, providers (names not public).

## 4 · Risk adjustment & coding accuracy (payer)
- **Reveleer** — AI risk-adjustment + quality/HEDIS for health plans; EVE™ AI assistant maps missed diagnoses to HCCs at ~99% accuracy; accelerates record retrieval up to 80%.
  - *Managed services:* **Hybrid** — AI platform + record-retrieval/coding services; reported ~3x ROI in year one.
  - *Clients:* health plans and risk-bearing providers (names largely not public).
- **Pareto Intelligence** — payer risk-adjustment & analytics (Pareto Hub data environment + actuarial/analytics services).
  - *Managed services:* **Hybrid** — analytics platform + services.
  - *Clients:* health plans (names not public).

---

### At-a-glance: who *runs the process* for payers (managed / BPaaS)
- **Yes:** Alaffia Health.
- **Hybrid (software + delegated/managed):** Cohere Health, Machinify, Codoxo, Lyric, Rialtic, Reveleer, Pareto Intelligence.
- **No (software the plan operates):** Anterior, Basys.ai, Itiliti Health, ClarisHealth, ClaimSphere, Amera, Curacel.

**Strategic read:** even on the payer side, most AI-natives ship **software**; only **Alaffia** (and Cohere's delegated option) truly *run* the operation. The **"large service provider that operates payer claims ops with AI inside"** lane is largely open — a differentiator worth naming.
