# AI-Native Companies in Healthcare Claims — Landscape

*Compiled June 2026. Scope: US-centric, companies built **AI-first** (legacy RCM incumbents such as Optum, R1, and Waystar are excluded). "AI-native" is a spectrum; funding figures are point-in-time and this space shifts monthly.*

> ⭐ = most relevant to an intake/referral + payer-call use case.

## 1 · Claims intake, referral & payer-call automation
- ⭐ **Tennr** — AI for the patient **referral/intake** front office: document intake plus voice AI that auto-calls payers for benefits investigations, documentation follow-up, and confirmations. ~10M docs/month; **$101M Series C** (~$605M valuation). *Closest analog to an intake agent.*
- ⭐ **Infinitus** — purpose-built **voice AI for payer-facing work**: eligibility, prior auth, **claim status**, EOB retrieval, appeals, and over-the-phone submissions. Collects 150+ data points per call.

## 2 · Autonomous medical coding (charts → claims)
- **Fathom** — autonomous coding for high-volume specialties (ED, radiology, primary care, surgery). #1 "Reducing the Cost of Care," 2025 KLAS Emerging Solutions Top 20.
- **CodaMetrix** — autonomous coding for hospitals/health systems; CMX CARE uses the *longitudinal* patient record for context, not just the episode.
- **Nym Health** — semantic / "clinical language" autonomous coder; strong ED product; Epic Showroom "Fully Autonomous Coding" designation.
- **Arintra** — charts → claims at ~96% code accuracy; reported ~43% cut in coding-related denials and ~11% reduction in undercoding.
- **RapidClaims · MediCodio · Maverick Medical AI** — newer AI-native coding / claims-automation entrants.

## 3 · Provider RCM, claims automation & denials
- **Candid Health** — full medical-claims-lifecycle automation; 200+ orgs; touchless claims reported above 95%.
- **Adonis** — AI **orchestration + agents** for RCM and denials; **$40M Series C** (2026), >$95M total; automated appeals reported to save ~$200–500 each.
- **AKASA** — generative AI across the revenue cycle (prior auth → CDI → coding → claims), trained on each health system's own data.
- **Sohar Health** — API-first front-end RCM (eligibility + claim accuracy), ~95% automation.
- **Syntra · Taiga · LunaBill** — AI billing/claims for private practices; LunaBill is voice AI for billing follow-up calls.
- **Aegis** — automates insurance **denial appeals** end-to-end for providers.

## 4 · Prior authorization
- **Cohere Health** — market leader; ~47M payer-provider interactions/yr, 85% real-time approvals; ~$200M raised; expanding into payment integrity and policy management.
- **Anterior** (formerly Co:Helm) — AI for health plans: prior auth, payment integrity, risk adjustment; **$40M** raised; deployed at payers (e.g., Geisinger Health Plan).

## 5 · Payer-side: payment integrity & claims adjudication
- **Machinify** — unified pre-pay + post-pay **payment-integrity** platform across the claim lifecycle.
- **Anomaly (Anomaly Insights)** — AI payer-intelligence for denials, underpayments, and adjudication deviations; **+$17M** (2026); deployed across 20+ health systems.
- **Alaffia Health** — AI + expert clinicians for end-to-end claims operations (utilization management, payment integrity, appeals).
- **Curacel · Amera** — AI claims processing/automation for payers and TPAs; Amera structures messy PDF/EDI/non-standard claim inputs into clean data.

## 6 · Consumer / patient-side denial appeals
- **Counterforce Health** — free AI appeal-letter generator; ~70% appeal success rate; copies state regulators on filings to flag denial patterns.
- **Claimable** — AI-generated appeals (~$40/letter); signing deals with drugmakers and health systems to appeal on patients' behalf.
