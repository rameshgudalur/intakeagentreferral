The Shift & What They'll Learn
From rules to judgment. SOPs teach the rules; they don't build the judgment to apply them when a provider has no slot inside the SLA, a worker only speaks Spanish, or the auth is missing. Associates do the real job on realistic cases, get instant coaching, and reflect on why.
  • Read a PT referral & spot what's needed (provider, appointment, transport, interpreter, auth)
  • Schedule within the jurisdiction SLA, choosing the right provider under real constraints
  • Run professional, empathetic calls — with the injured worker and the PT clinic
  • Triage curveballs — missing auth, SLA risk, no-shows, cancellations: solve vs. escalate
  • Document a clean, defensible coordination hand-off
