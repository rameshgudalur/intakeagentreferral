SOP-Driven Agent · Maturity Roadmap

Your SOPs run the agent — today, and increasingly over time

The agent operates on your Standard Operating Procedures. Today every decision is applied per case and traced to its SOP clause and the regulation behind it. The roadmap deepens that from configured‑from‑your‑SOPs → generated‑from‑your‑SOPs → a governed learning loop where the SOPs improve from the agent's own evidence.

Horizon 3

SOP‑Learning (Closed Loop)

2–3 years
What it is
Decision history + human overrides surface patterns → the agent proposes SOP refinements (new rules, tuned thresholds, edge cases) → humans approve under change control.
The shift
The evolving SOP becomes the living source of truth; the decision↔SOP record is the audit trail. SOPs and decisions co‑evolve.
Maturity
Self‑improving · evidence‑grounded
Guardrails — what keeps it defensible at every horizon
👤 Human‑governed SOP changes
The agent proposes, the Operations Lead approves under change control. The agent never silently rewrites its own SOPs — so it's never "grading its own homework," and the audit trail stays trustworthy.
⚖️ SOP‑led, regulation‑bounded
SOPs drive operational decisions, but can never override the regulatory floor (CMS / state WC). Any conflict is escalated, not auto‑resolved — compliance is the hard boundary.
