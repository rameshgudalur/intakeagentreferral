SOP-Driven

Your SOPs → The Agent's Rules

The agent doesn't run on generic logic — it runs on your Standard Operating Procedures. Each SOP clause is compiled into the agent's structured rule library, gap criteria, and escalation logic, every decision traceable back to the SOP and its cited authority. Your SOPs stay the governed source of truth; the agent is configured from them.

Three ways your SOPs drive the agent
1 · Compile
SOP → deterministic rules
Rule-like clauses become entries in the OWL knowledge graph — deterministic, citation-cited, 100% auditable. This is the "the rule says so, here's the source" layer.
2 · Retrieve
SOP text at runtime
For nuanced, non-deterministic guidance the agent retrieves the relevant SOP passage at decision time — so judgment calls follow your written procedure, not a guess.
3 · Teach
SOP → training simulator
The same SOPs power the coached intake simulator, so associates learn your procedures experientially — people and agent run off one source of truth.

Governance — SOPs as the source of truth

How changes flow

SOPs are the versioned, governed source. When the Ops Lead updates a procedure, the change runs through review — the agent's rule is regenerated from the new SOP, re-validated, and only then published live. Every rule the agent fires links back to its SOP clause and cited authority, so an auditor can trace decision → rule → SOP → regulation in one path. Nothing in the agent is a black box or hand-coded around the SOPs.
