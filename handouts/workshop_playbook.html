Design → Demo → Deploy — Run-of-Show

June 10, 2026Workshop Day

A section-by-section playbook for the facilitator. Each session gives the Goal, The Play (how you run it), the Collateral to have ready, and a Tip.

Hands-on markers: 🧪 Live Lab · 🃏 Card Sort · 🛠 Build Live. Breaks at 10:45 & 2:30; lunch 12:20 (good for 1:1 security/IT questions).

9:00 · 30m👋 Welcome & Objectives
GoalShared definition of success; agree the decisions to leave with.
PlayOpen with the client’s own pain (backlog, rekeying). Put a “What does good look like?” poster up; each attendee posts one outcome on a sticky note. Group them live — that becomes the day’s scorecard.
Collateralprinted agenda run-sheet · objectives worksheet · attendee name cards · sticky notes + markers.
TipDon’t demo yet. Make their goals the frame everything else pays off against.
9:30 · 45m▶ Live Demonstration🧪 Live Lab
GoalThe “is it real?” moment — answered on screen, on their data.
PlayRun a real referral packet end-to-end (extraction → ICD conflict → gap detection → outreach → routing). Hand out printed copies of the same packet; attendees scan the QR and run one at the stations.
Collateralprinted referral packets · QR cards to the live demo (Railway URL) · laptop/tablet stations · recap card.
TipUse a packet with a deliberate gap so gap-detection visibly fires — that’s the credibility builder.
10:15 · 30m🧩 How It Works — Architecture & Workflow
GoalShow the design is principled, not a black box.
PlayGather at the architecture poster. Trace one referral’s path with your finger: intake → AI extraction → OWL rule engine → completeness → route. Tie each stop to what they just saw.
Collateralarchitecture + workflow poster (foam board) · data-flow one-pager · 25-field episode-record sample.
TipKeep it a walk-the-wall, not slides. Let them point and ask.
☕ Break · 10:45 · 15 min
11:00 · 40m🛡 Trust & Human-in-the-Loop
GoalAnswer “can we trust it?” head-on.
PlayWalk the four-layer-defense one-pager, then live-trigger a low-confidence case so the agent stops and routes to the human queue. Pass around a printed audit trail for one episode.
Collateralfour-layer-defense one-pager · sample audit-trail printout · escalation-queue screenshot card.
Tip“Stump the agent” — invite a tricky case. When it escalates instead of guessing, trust is earned.
11:40 · 40m🔌 Integration & Knowledge Graph🃏 Card Sort
GoalProve it fits their stack with no rip-and-replace.
PlayHand each table the 24 rule cards; sort by payer/scenario. Show the same rules firing in the agent. Walk the integration one-pager (API/RPA/file) against their systems.
Collateraldeck of 24 CMS rule cards · integration-patterns one-pager · knowledge-graph poster.
TipCapture which intake channels and case-mgmt/EHR they use — it seeds the Discovery phase.
🍴 Lunch · 12:20 · 50 min — informal time for security/IT to ask the hard questions 1:1
1:10 · 40m🏢 Security, Compliance & Enterprise Readiness
GoalClear the #1 blocker — PHI/HIPAA.
PlayWork the HIPAA/PHI checklist line by line; walk the data-boundary diagram (PHI never leaves their boundary); hand the security lead the draft BAA summary to take away.
CollateralHIPAA/PHI checklist · draft BAA summary · security-architecture one-pager.
TipGet the security/IT stakeholder in the room for this slot. Their yes unblocks everything.
1:50 · 40m💰 The Business Case & ROI
GoalTurn “impressive” into “justified.”
PlayPlug their volume into the ROI calculator live; show $0.032/referral against time saved and throughput. Let them see the payback on their own numbers.
CollateralROI calculator handout (editable) · token-economics one-pager · cost-per-referral card.
TipAsk for monthly referral volume up front so the number on screen is theirs.
☕ Break · 2:30 · 15 min
2:45 · 40m🚀 Deployment & Rollout Plan🛠 Build Live
GoalMake the path concrete and low-risk.
PlayWalk the 8–12 week timeline poster (Discovery → Setup → Test → Pilot/Go-Live). Then build live: make a small payer-rule tweak and push it — show it on the public site minutes later.
Collateral8–12 week timeline poster · environment & integration checklist · deployment runbook outline.
TipPre-stage the change so the live build is quick and can’t flop in the room.
3:25 · 30m🔗 Roadmap & Agent Ecosystem
GoalShow it’s a platform, not a point tool.
PlayReveal the 8-agent value-chain poster; mark Agent 1 (intake) live, scheduling next. Invite them to point at which agent solves their next-biggest pain.
Collateral8-agent value-chain poster · roadmap one-pager · scheduling-agent teaser card.
TipLet their priority shape the roadmap order — co-ownership drives the deal.
3:55 · 35m✅ Decisions, Owners & Next Steps
GoalWalk out with commitments, not impressions.
PlayFill the decision log on the wall: pilot scope, success metrics (from the morning stickies), named owners, dates. Get a signature on the pilot-scope sign-off.
Collateraldecision log + owners (RACI) sheet · pilot-scope sign-off form · success-metrics worksheet.
TipNothing leaves the room without an owner and a date next to it.
