Make It Tangible — Props

  • Poster walls, not slides — architecture & value chain on foam boards; sticky-note gallery walk.
  • Printed referral packets — mark up by hand, then watch the agent process the same one.
  • Knowledge-graph rule cards — sort the 24 CMS rules, then see them fire in the agent.
  • Live lab — run the agent yourself (laptops + QR); “stump” it, watch the escalation gate.
  • Build-it-live — a rule change pushed live in the room, on the public site minutes later.
  • Fill the episode record — 25 fields by hand vs. the agent’s auto-fill. Feel the gap.

Every Consideration, Covered

  • Security & compliance — PHI, HIPAA, signed BAA, encryption, access, audit, retention.
  • Trust & quality — accuracy targets, confidence gates, human review, failure handling.
  • Integration — fax/voice/portal in; EHR & case-mgmt out; API/RPA/file.
  • Operations — monitoring, SLAs, on-call, runbooks, throughput at scale.
  • Commercials — pricing, ROI baseline, token cost, pilot vs. production.
  • Change management — training, parallel-run, manual fallback, adoption plan.
